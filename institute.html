What ACDI is

Education and credentialing, integrated.

ACDI is an advanced professional education and credentialing institute that develops demonstrated competence through evidence-informed, experiential learning.

Education and credentialing are not separate purposes here. The credential represents successful completion and validation of the entire experience, which is why it requires rigorous preparation, firsthand experiential and clinical learning, and assessment.

The “Advanced” in ACDI

Minimum standards are a floor, not the objective.

Healthcare design affects human health and care delivery, yet design practice does not consistently require the structured learning from consequences that healthcare itself demands.

ACDI develops the judgment to use available evidence, resources, and experience to optimize healthcare environments, oriented toward clinical reality and improved efficiency, efficacy, economy, experience, and outcomes.

Evidence and research

Research supports the model. Education remains the purpose.

Evidence and experience inform each other in a translational feedback loop.

  1. 1Evidence informs the curriculum and methods.
  2. 2Participants apply that knowledge in complex environments.
  3. 3Experience generates new observations and questions.
  4. 4Those observations can feed back into the evidence base.

Institutional principles

Five commitments that govern decisions.

  1. 1

    Protect educational and credential integrity.

    ACDI credentials represent demonstrated learning and competence, not attendance, prior reputation, payment, or affiliation.

  2. 2

    Preserve firsthand experiential and clinical learning.

    ACDI will not substitute passive or virtual education for required experiential and clinical components simply to make the program easier or more scalable.

  3. 3

    Ground learning in evidence and translate experience into professional judgment.

    Experience alone is insufficient. Every substantive learning experience has a defensible educational purpose and connects what participants encounter to what they subsequently do.

  4. 4

    Protect institutional independence, ethics, and credibility.

    No sponsor, affiliate, employer, partner, founder, or other stakeholder may purchase or improperly influence curriculum, assessment, credentialing, findings, endorsement, or Fellowship.

  5. 5

    Grow only when quality can grow with us.

    ACDI expands only when it can preserve educational quality, experiential fidelity, assessment rigor, and institutional credibility.

How ACDI will grow

Validate, repeat, then establish.

ACDI will establish credibility before pursuing scale, and it will expand only as the evidence supports it.

  1. Alpha

    Validate

    Determine whether the learning model produces the participant transformation ACDI expects, and capture what must change.

  2. Beta

    Repeat

    Demonstrate that the experience can be reproduced with another cohort, without depending on one-time circumstances.

  3. Launch

    Establish

    Launch once ACDI can credibly explain what it is, what its credential represents, how its model works, and what evidence exists.

  4. Growth

    Grow

    Develop cohorts, partners, Fellows, and a growing evidence base, with scale following demonstrated capacity and credential integrity.

Validators

Professional societies and associations

Advance healthcare-design competency through rigorous experiential education and demonstrated competence.

ACDI intends to pursue recognized healthcare-simulation standards, independent credential review, respected advisory participation, and validation from relevant medical and clinical societies as well as design organizations.

Delivery partners

Simulation, clinical, and faculty partners

Simulation organizations, teaching hospitals and clinical sites, faculty, and subject-matter experts make the experiential model possible.

These relationships enable experiences that are difficult to reproduce through conventional professional education.

Industry

Product affiliates

Participate in a credible learning environment in which products, equipment, and environments can be understood in the context of clinical use.

The institutional boundary

Product affiliates may support, participate, learn, and receive appropriate recognition. They may not purchase ACDI certification, Fellowship, favorable educational findings, product endorsement, or control over curriculum or credentialing decisions.

Discuss product affiliation

Advanced practitioners

Fellows and alumni

Demonstrate that ACDI learning has been translated into disciplined professional application and evidence of impact.

Over time, Fellows and alumni may contribute as reviewers, proctors, faculty, mentors, and advisors.
